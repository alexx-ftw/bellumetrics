# Bellumetrics brand design

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Approved

## Decision

The platform will be renamed from **Commander Elo** to **Bellumetrics**.

- Platform name: **Bellumetrics**
- Tagline: **Military history, measured.**
- Primary ranking: **Commander Elo**

Bellumetrics is the umbrella brand for the structured historical wiki, sources, conflicts, commander profiles, graph exploration, simulations, and versioned rankings. Commander Elo remains the name of one ranking within the platform.

## Rationale

Commander Elo describes the current prototype but is too narrow for the intended product. Bellumetrics combines the military-history meaning of *bellum* with quantitative analysis and can cover commanders, battles, campaigns, sources, networks, and future models without tying the platform to a single algorithm.

The name is international, pronounceable in Spanish and English, serious but memorable, and compatible with the project's historical-analytical visual direction.

## Usage

- Product heading: **Bellumetrics**
- Product tagline: **Military history, measured.**
- Ranking label: **Commander Elo**
- Example: “Napoleon ranks third in Bellumetrics' Commander Elo.”

Do not use Commander Elo as the name of the whole platform after the migration.

## Migration scope

A later implementation plan will cover:

1. Rename visible product copy and metadata.
2. Rename the GitHub repository from `commander-elo` to `bellumetrics`.
3. Move the public deployment to the new path or domain.
4. Preserve redirects and references from old URLs where the hosting platform permits.
5. Keep historical links and commit history intact.
6. Check repository, package, environment, documentation, social, and deployment identifiers before changing them.

The rename does not change the historical data model, collaborative-wiki design, Commander Elo formula, or moderation rules.
